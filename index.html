Instruction-conditioned grasping on a Toyota HSR

GPT-4o reasons about what the task needs and which part to grasp. LangSAM finds the object, but asked for a part it returns 86 to 97 percent of the whole object, so I find the part geometrically in the point cloud instead. Contact-GraspNet proposes grasps on that part, a collision-aware planner picks where the base should stand, and visual servoing from the hand camera lines up the final approach.

The core finding: language can name part-level constraints that segmentation cannot resolve.

Franka Panda in PyBullet. TQC with hindsight experience replay.

The first policy learned to fall slowly. A different algorithm, no observation normalisation and a fixed environment version turned it into a gait.

About

I came to robotics from AI and machine learning, and stayed for the part where models meet the physical world.

My B.Tech at NMIMS was in artificial intelligence and machine learning. At Heriot-Watt I moved onto real hardware: the Toyota HSR at the National Robotarium, supervised by Dr Mauro Dragone. I like problems that only show up on a real robot, like a camera with all-zero intrinsics or a wrist command silently rejected at its joint limit.

Outside the lab I write poetry and make visual art, and I led the creative department of the Atrangi Fine Arts Club at NMIMS. That's where my interest in robots that share space with people comes from. It's why I care how a robot hands over a knife, not only whether it can pick one up.

I'm based in Edinburgh.
